ReelShift Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

ReelShift is a browser extension that automatically advances to the next YouTube Short or Facebook Reel when the current video ends.

Data we store

ReelShift stores only your extension preferences on your device using the browser storage API. Preferences may include:

  • Whether ReelShift is enabled
  • Whether YouTube Shorts support is enabled
  • Whether Facebook Reels support is enabled
  • Delay before advancing to the next video
  • Whether auto-advance pauses while you are typing or commenting

Data we do not collect

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No video content, comments, or watch history uploads
  • No account login information
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with third parties

Network activity

ReelShift does not send your settings or browsing activity to a ReelShift server. The extension only interacts with the YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels pages you open in order to detect video end and trigger the next video.

Permissions

  • storage: required to save your preferences locally in the browser.
  • Host access to YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels: required so content scripts can detect when a short-form video ends and advance to the next one.

Children

ReelShift is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new “Last updated” date.
